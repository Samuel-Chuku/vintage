05 / Mechanism

The chain already dates every shielded spend.

01 The tree

A fingerprint per block

Every block ends with a root: one fingerprint of every shielded coin that exists at that moment.

02 The anchor

Every spend names one

A spend must point at one of those roots and prove its coins are inside it. Consensus rejects it otherwise.

03 The check
Mainnet

Older root, older coins

If the named root is older than the event, the coins were already there. Nothing else about them is revealed.

No blocklist. No new cryptography. A rule Zcash already enforces on every spend.

06 / Events

One proof, any dated event.

One deposit proves it predates every event after its anchor, all at once. The registry labels block heights and nothing else: no addresses, no people, and anyone can check against any height they choose.

HackLive

Exploit inflows

A German finance ministry draft, not yet law, would end the one-year tax exemption for coins bought from 2027 and keep it for earlier ones. If it passes, prove the date without handing a platform your keys.

Fresh coins · Payer side
Nothing revealed

Ask your payer to send with Vintage

Coins you received after an event can't be dated by age. But if the person paying you uses an older anchor, the payment itself is provably clean, and you know it the moment it lands.

$ vintage request --to <your address> \

Fresh coins · Carry it forward
Optional · one key, one rail

A single-use receipt account

Receive the Vintage payment into a fresh account with its own seed, deposit from it, and let the exit rail check the link with that account's viewing key. It is optional, and the only Vintage feature that discloses anything. What it discloses:

The receipt account's viewing key, therefore the funding payments, the amounts received, the receipt address, and the deposit. Nothing from the user's other accounts. The viewing key cannot spend. Rotate to a fresh receipt account for every use.

The rail can run the check itself with vintage link verify, so the key goes to nobody else. A zero-knowledge version that discloses no key is on the roadmap.

09 / Limits

What it proves, and what it doesn't.

Proves
  • Every Ironwood coin the deposit spends was already in the pool before the event block.
  • It holds by consensus. Nobody can fake an older anchor.
  • It covers every event after the anchor at once.
Doesn't
  • Date coins received after the event. They need the payer-side path.
  • Cover transparent, Sapling or Orchard inputs.
  • Say who sent the coins or how much. It only rules out known events.
  • Hide that you chose to prove it. An old anchor is visible on chain, so use it only when you deposit somewhere that already knows you.
  • Make it mandatory. It's yours to offer, never a rail's to demand from everyone.
10 / Integrate

One request before you hold a deposit.

An exit rail calls the verifier with the deposit txid and the event. A pass means every coin in the deposit predates that event. It does not identify anyone and it is not a source-of-funds report.

Run the verifier yourself, or call ours. Either way the answer comes from public chain data, so you can check it independently.
